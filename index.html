03 / Timeline

When the current buildings opened

One dot is one venue on the year its current building opened, or reopened after a rebuild. of have a reliable opening year on record. Choose a program to light it up.

The peaks follow the events chosen for the study. They do not describe when stadiums were built in general.

04 / Method

Four readings of the same circle

Each venue is described with four readings, taken from the places mapped around it and from the street network that connects them.

D1

Density

How many places are mapped inside the circle, and where they concentrate.

D2

Distance

How far each place is from the stadium along the streets, not in a straight line.

D3

Diversity

How mixed the kinds of places are: food, retail, health, education and others.

D4

Destinations

Which kinds of places are present, and which are absent.

Limits

The observatory shows what is mapped, which is not always what exists. A circle with few dots can mean a quiet area or thin platform coverage, so a low count is not evidence of an empty city. The readings describe what stands near a stadium; they do not show that the stadium produced it. Venues were not all collected in the same year, and each venue page states its date where one is on record. The site publishes counts per cell, per kind and per distance ring, not individual place records.
